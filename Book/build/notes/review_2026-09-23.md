## Priority summary

### A. Wrong or unsafe technical content (fix first)
1. ch02 — practical 5 "নিরাপদ অঞ্চলে ছিদ্র করা": the "safe zones" are where the cables ARE; the task tells students to drill into them.
2. ch01/ch02 — Prove–Test–Prove practical uses a neon লাইন টেস্টার; ch02's own text says neon cannot prove dead; starter kit has no two-pole tester.
3. ch15 — "N আর আর্থ বার মিটারের পরে কখনো এক নয়": wrong for TN-C-S (one link at the origin is required).
4. ch12 — practical 5 (remove 21-22 interlock wire) doesn't say Q1 OFF → possible phase-to-phase short.
5. ch14 — "A16 = 16 A AC-3" (ABB A16 is 17 A); interlock test (practical 3 / সারণি ১৪.৬) removes the wrong 21-22 and proves nothing.
6. ch04 — সারণি ৪.৫: series (test) board shown with lamp and socket in PARALLEL.
7. ch06 + ch15 — "ডাইভার্সিটি ফ্যাক্টর ০.৭ দিয়ে গুণ": that is demand factor; diversity factor ≥ 1.
8. ch01 — body resistance (dry 100 kΩ, "সাধারণ শুকনা হাত 10 kΩ") understates hazard vs IEC 60479 (~1–2 kΩ at 230 V).
9. ch07 — run-capacitor formula C = 10⁵·P·Eff/(V²f): no source, physically odd, gives ~half the real value for a fan.
10. ch07 — discharge a big capacitor by shorting with a screwdriver (use a bleeder resistor).
11. ch08 vs ch12 — AC-3 "breaks 8 × Ie" vs AC-4 "breaks 6 × Ie": two different bases (capacity test vs normal duty), reads as AC-3 harsher.
12. ch11 vs ch12 — "electrical interlock doesn't protect against welded contacts" vs "21-22 mirror contact does".
13. ch17 — "switch into NVR PoE port → power from both sides": PSEs don't power each other; storage formula mixes decimal/binary units.
14. ch17 — DORI Detect = "শনাক্ত", Identify = "সনাক্তকরণ" — same word, two spellings, opposite ends of the scale.
15. ch16 — practical: measure Isc through a multimeter (blows a 10 A jack on the 12.28 A module used in the chapter).
16. ch05 — ordinary clamp meter on L+N to find leakage for "বারবার ব্রেকার ট্রিপ".
17. ch08 — "কন্ট্রোল ও পাওয়ার সার্কিট বৈদ্যুতিকভাবে আলাদা" (false with 230 V control from L–N).
